An implied value is this page's own claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It predicts no launch: a protocol here records no token, which is not proof it has none. Gas, drop share and carry are stated assumptions, not snapshot figures.

Known blind spot: launch odds are base rates from look-alikes, not forecasts — they do not condition on having survived tokenless this long. Old, tokenless names are where the model is most likely wrong.
